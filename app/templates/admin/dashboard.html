User Manual: How Anyone Can Connect Facebook & Instagram in 2 Minutes
Method A: 1-Click Auto-Discover via Token (Fastest)
  1. Open Meta Graph API Explorer .
  2. Select your Meta Business App in the top-right dropdown.
  3. Under User or Page, pick Get Page Access Token and check these permissions (click to copy):
    pages_manage_posts pages_read_engagement pages_show_list business_management instagram_basic instagram_content_publish
  4. Click Generate Access Token, select your Facebook Page, and copy the token.
  5. Paste the token below and click "Auto-Discover & Connect"! (If Instagram is not linked yet, it automatically connects Facebook Only!)
How to Link Instagram with Your Facebook Page (Optional)
  1. Switch Instagram to Business: Open the Instagram mobile app → Profile > Edit Profile > Switch to Professional Account → select Business.
  2. Link to Your Facebook Page: Open your Facebook Page on desktop → click Manage / Settings → Linked Accounts → Instagram → click Connect Account and log in to Instagram.
  3. Re-Run Auto-Discover: Once linked, paste your token below and click Auto-Discover & Connect—both Facebook and Instagram will connect automatically!
