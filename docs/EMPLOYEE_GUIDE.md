## 2. Adding a New Furniture Item (Step-by-Step)

```
[Tap "Add Item"] ➡️ [Snap 1-3 Photos] ➡️ [Enter Title & Price] ➡️ [Tap "Save & Publish"]
```

### Step 1: Open the Camera
- Tap the bright yellow **"Add New Product"** button or the round camera icon at the bottom of your screen.
- Tap **"Open Camera"** to take a fresh photo with your phone camera, or tap **"Gallery"** to pick photos you already took.
- **Tip:** Take 2 to 4 photos:
  1. Front full view (this will automatically be the main cover photo).
  2. Side / 45-degree angle.
  3. Close-up of fabric, wood grain, or unique details.
  4. Label or tags showing brand / dimensions if available.

### Step 2: Enter Product Details
- **Title:** e.g., *"Modern Gray Sectional Sofa with Chaise"*
- **Outlet Price:** Enter what the store is selling it for (e.g. `299.00`).
- **Retail Price (Optional):** Enter original retail price (e.g. `799.00`). The system will automatically calculate and display the savings for customers!
- **Category:** Pick from dropdown (Living Room, Bedroom, Dining, etc.).
- **Condition:** Brand New, Like New, Floor Model, etc.
- **Auto-Generate Description:** Tap the magic wand button 🪄 to automatically generate an engaging description including store pickup details!

### Step 3: Choose Publishing Channels
Under **"3. Publish Online"**, make sure the switches are toggled **ON**:
- ✅ **Facebook Business Page:** Automatically posts photos and description to the Overstock Outlet Page feed.
- ✅ **Instagram Business:** Automatically publishes to the store Instagram feed.
- ✅ **Facebook Marketplace (Assisted):** Prepares a 1-tap copy pack for Marketplace.

### Step 4: Tap "Save & Publish"
- Tap the **Save & Publish** button.
- You will see a green confirmation toast notification.

## 3. How to Post to Facebook Marketplace (The Assisted Guide)

> **Why do we use the Assistant?**  
> Facebook protects Marketplace accounts and requires a human user to confirm the post. Our system makes this super fast!

When you publish an item with Marketplace checked, the **Marketplace Assistant** opens automatically:

1. **Tap the blue button:** `"1. Open Facebook Marketplace"` — this opens Facebook's official item creator.
2. **Upload Photos:** In Facebook, tap "Add Photos" and choose the photos in order (1, 2, 3...).
3. **1-Tap Copy:** Return to our assistant screen and tap:
   - Tap **"Copy Title"** ➡️ Paste in Facebook.
   - Tap **"Copy Price"** ➡️ Paste in Facebook.
   - Tap **"Copy Description"** ➡️ Paste in Facebook.
4. **Location:** Verify location is set to **Baltimore, MD**.
5. **Publish:** Tap "Publish" in Facebook.
6. **Done!** Return to our assistant screen and tap **"Mark as Published on Marketplace"**.

## 4. Helpful Tips & Troubleshooting

- **Duplicate Warning:** If you see an orange banner saying *"Potential duplicate detected"*, someone may have already added this item recently. Check the inventory list first before re-adding.
- **Need to Mark an Item Sold?**
  1. Go to the dashboard.
  2. Search for the item title or SKU.
  3. Tap **Edit**.
  4. Change Availability to **Sold**.
  5. Tap **Save**.
- **Accidental Typos:** You can edit any product at any time by tapping the pencil icon ✏️ on the item card.
